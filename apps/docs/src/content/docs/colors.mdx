Colours come in two layers. The **palette** is the raw ramps; nothing in a component reads it
directly. The **classes** (`bg-ws-…`, `text-ws-…`, `border-ws-…`) are what components use; they
point at the palette and switch with dark mode. Every swatch on this page reads the live token,
so switch the site theme to see the dark values. To change any of them, see
[Theming](/docs/theming).

## Palette

Primitives are named `--ws-colors-light-<family>-<step>` (and `-dark-` for the dark ramps).
Override the `brand` ramps, both of them, for a re-brand: [Theming](/docs/theming#re-brand) has the
recipe.

## Background

`bg-ws-*`. Static tokens are the page and its containers; dynamic tokens are interactive
surfaces with `default`, `hover`, `pressed` and `disabled` states.
