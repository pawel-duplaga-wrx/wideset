# Code of Conduct

Wideset follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies in every project space: issues, discussions, pull requests, reviews, and anywhere
you represent the project.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and focus on the work. Harassment,
personal attacks and discriminatory language are not accepted.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers at `wojciech.kaczor@widelab.co`. Reports are
handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, or block contributors
who break this code, following the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
